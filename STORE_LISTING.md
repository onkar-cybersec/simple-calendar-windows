## Product name

Dayframe Calendar

## Short description

A clean glass calendar with Indian, U.S., and local festival holidays.

## Description

Dayframe Calendar is a focused desktop calendar for Windows with a polished transparent interface and an easy-to-read monthly view.

See Indian and U.S. public holidays alongside major Hindu and local festivals. Holiday dates are clearly distinguished while the seven weekday headers use gentle rainbow colors for fast visual scanning.

Dayframe Calendar works offline, does not require an account, and does not collect analytics or personal data. Its Windows live tile keeps the current date visible from Start.

## Features

- Clean monthly calendar with a transparent glass background
- Indian and U.S. public holidays
- Major Hindu and local Indian festivals
- Bold, high-contrast dates and weekday labels
- Previous- and next-month mini calendars
- Windows live tile with the current date
- Light and dark system-theme support
- Offline operation with no account or tracking
